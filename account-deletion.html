Delete your QTRIG account

QTRIG app · operated by Štěpán Včelák · valid from 17 October 2026

You can delete your QTRIG account yourself at any time — directly in the app, or by asking us without the app. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

1. Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open the app and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Tap Settings (the gear in the right-hand column of buttons) → More at the top → About.
  3. In the Account section at the bottom, tap Delete account…
  4. Type your account password and confirm with Really delete the account.

With Pro, the same button is also on the Where I work (workspaces) screen.

2. Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in, send a request at github.com/stepanvcelak11/ar-geodet/issues (or via “Write to the author” in the app if you still have it). Give your account code (the eight characters you got when you created the account) and the name you signed up with. To check that the account is really yours we may ask for one more detail (for example when and on which phone the account was created). We handle the request within 30 days at the latest and let you know the same way.

What is deleted

  • The account — name, account code, password hash, optional contact, plan.
  • Account backups of your points, jobs and settings.
  • Passkeys (“Sign in with Face ID”) registered to the account. Remove the passkey from your phone's keychain as well (iPhone: Settings → Passwords; Android: Google Password Manager).
  • Your own space on the server, including points and jobs synced into it.
  • Memberships in companies you were invited to, and your usage records and error reports in them.
  • Messages from the app's author to your account.

What remains and why

  • Points and jobs on your phone — they are yours. Remove them by clearing the app's data on the phone (Settings → Apps → QTRIG → Storage → Clear data; on iPhone by removing the icon from the home screen).
  • Points and jobs in a company you were a member of — they belong to the company, not to one person (just as when you leave the company). Your name stays on them as the record of who measured the point.
  • Pro orders and payments (made outside Google Play) — accounting records that must be kept by law; the link to the account is removed on deletion.
  • Error reports that can no longer be linked to your name contain only the type of phone, the app version and the error text, and are deleted automatically after 90 days. Anonymous phone accuracy statistics (phone model and figures, no account) are not linked to you and stay.

If you are the last admin of a company with other members, the app asks you to hand over the admin role or leave the company before deletion — otherwise the company would be left without an admin.
