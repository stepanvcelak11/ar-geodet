# Tester invitation — Google Play closed test (English)

English version of `play/pozvanka-testeri.md`. Replace `LINK` with the **opt-in link** from Play Console
(*Testing → Closed testing → Testers → Copy link*). Invite 16–18 people, not exactly 12 — if the number
of opted-in testers drops below 12, the 14-day window starts again. Every tester needs their own Google
account and a real Android phone.

---

## A) Long version — e-mail / message

> **Subject: 5 minutes of your help — testing my app QTRIG on Google Play**
>
> Hi,
>
> I'm finishing **QTRIG**, a field app for surveyors: finding survey control points in augmented reality,
> setting out with chainage, cadastre, field measurements and coordinate export. It all works offline too.
>
> Before Google lets it into the store, it has to run in a closed test for fourteen days with at least
> 12 people. Could you please:
>
> **1.** On an Android phone, open this link and tap “Become a tester”:
> LINK
>
> **2.** On the same page, follow the link to Google Play and **install the app**.
>
> **3.** On first start the app asks for an **account** — tap “Create account”. It's three fields and no
> e-mail. You can't get in without it. Save the password; it can't be recovered yet.
>
> **4.** And the main thing: **keep the app installed for at least 14 days** and don't leave the test.
> You don't have to use it — it just needs to be installed. If someone removes it, the whole 14-day
> window starts again, so this really helps.
>
> If you try it in the field, any feedback is welcome — what's unclear, what's missing, what crashed.
> Just write to me directly.
>
> Thanks a lot,
> Štěpán

---

## B) Short version — SMS / WhatsApp

> Hi, I'm finishing an app for surveyors and Google wants 12 people to test it for 14 days. Can you help?
> On Android open LINK → “Become a tester” → install from Play → on first start tap **“Create account”**
> (three fields, no e-mail). Then please just **don't uninstall it for 14 days** — no need to use it. Thanks!

---

## C) Reminder after a few days

> Hi, thanks for joining the QTRIG test. Just a reminder: **please keep the app for another ~10 days** —
> Google counts an unbroken period and if someone drops out it starts from zero. I'll let you know when it's done. Thanks!

---

## Questions to ask testers (for the production access form)

1. Did the app start and did you see the map with your position?
2. Did the camera work in the AR view, and did the phone allow location?
3. Did you try to create a point? Was it clear how?
4. Did anything crash, freeze, or was anything hard to read in the sun?
5. Was the English clear everywhere? Did you see any Czech text?
