Privacy Policy

QTRIG app · last updated 17 October 2026

Summary

QTRIG has no advertising and no third-party tracking. Your work — points, jobs, photos, settings — is stored on your device (the browser's localStorage / IndexedDB). It leaves the device only in the cases described below.

You need an account to use the app (a name and a password; the app gives you an eight-character account code). The app's server receives only: account data, anonymous usage records, error reports, the automatic backup of your points (which you can turn off) and whatever you send yourself (messages to the author, point sync within a company). You can delete your account at any time.

Location (GPS)

Your location is processed on the device: to show your position on the map, to guide you to points and for measurements. Coordinates leave the device only as part of a request when you ask for data from a third party (for example survey control points from a national mapping agency, map data, a place search or the weather forecast for your area). These requests only serve to answer your request; nothing from them is stored outside your device. Your location is not sent to the app's server.

Points you measure yourself contain coordinates. They stay on the device, except for the account backup and company sync described below.

Camera

The camera image is used only to display augmented reality (AR) and for features such as reading coordinates from a photo (OCR). Everything is processed on the device. Images and photos are not uploaded anywhere.

Microphone and voice notes

The microphone turns on only when you record a voice note or use the acoustic rangefinder (which measures the echo on the device and stores nothing). Voice notes are saved on your device. If speech-to-text is on (it can be switched off in the tool), the audio is processed by the speech recognition built into your browser, which on most devices sends the audio to the browser or system vendor (Google for Chrome and Android, Apple for Safari and iOS) under their privacy policy. With speech-to-text off, the recording stays on your phone.

Sensors (compass, gyroscope)

Device orientation is used only to draw the AR scene and the compass. It never leaves the device.

Account and the app's server

The app's server runs on Cloudflare (Workers and the D1 database). It stores:

  • account — the name you choose, the account code, a password verifier (a PBKDF2 hash, never the password itself), plan (Basics / Pro), time of sign-up and last sign-in, app version and type of phone (e.g. “iPhone”);
  • passkey (optional, “Sign in with Face ID / fingerprint”) — only the public key and its identifier. The private key stays in your phone's keychain (Apple iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager);
  • optional contact (phone or e-mail), only if you fill it in so the author can reply to you;
  • usage records — sign-in time, which tool was opened, how many points were added or changed (point and job identifiers, without coordinates and without point contents) and an anonymous device identifier derived from the browser. Used to see what is used in the app and to find bugs; deleted after 12 months;
  • error reports — error text, file and line, app version, type of phone and your name in the app; deleted after 90 days;
  • messages to the author (bug reports, ratings, Pro requests) that you send yourself, including the text and contact if you give one;
  • account backup — so you do not lose your work when the phone deletes app data, the app sends a compressed backup of your points, jobs and settings (no photos, no voice notes, no sign-in details) to your account once a day and after every 20 new points. The server keeps the two latest backups. You can turn it off in Settings → Backup and maintenance; the backups are deleted with your account;
  • phone accuracy statistics (optional) — after a “Field test”, the app can send only the phone model and the measured accuracy figures, without location and without your account, to show how other people with the same phone measure. It can be turned off in the Field test / My accuracy tool;
  • Pro orders (outside Google Play only) — order number, amount and payment status; these are accounting records.

This data is seen only by the operator, for running and maintaining the app and for answering your messages. It is never sold or passed on and is never used for advertising.

Companies and shared points (optional)

If you use the app in a company, you can join its space with an invitation code. In that case:

  • your membership (name, role) is shown to the company admin;
  • the company admin can also see the company's usage records (who measured what and when);
  • if point sync is on, the company's points and jobs are stored on the server and shared between the company's devices.

Points and jobs in a company belong to the company. When you leave the company or delete your account they stay there (with your name on the points you measured). You can leave a company at any time.

Deleting your account and data

You delete your account and everything that belongs to it on the server directly in the app (Settings → More → About → Delete account) or by a request without the app. The steps and the exact list of what is deleted and what remains are on the Account deletion page. Data on the phone is removed by clearing the app's / browser's data.

Third-party services

To show maps and download survey data, the app talks to these services (like any web request, they see your IP address):

  • National mapping and cadastral agencies — survey control points, cadastral maps, orthophotos and terrain heights for the country you work in: ČÚZK (Czechia), GKÚ / ÚGKK SR (Slovakia), BEV (Austria), swisstopo (Switzerland), Kadaster / PDOK (Netherlands), IGN (France), IGN (Spain), GUGiK (Poland), state surveying offices in Germany, and others listed in the app under each country;
  • QTRIG map data — the app's own map built from OpenStreetMap data (© OpenStreetMap contributors, Protomaps), served through the app's server on Cloudflare; map fonts from protomaps.github.io;
  • OpenStreetMap services (Overpass, Photon by Komoot) — map features around you and place search (the text you search for);
  • Open-Meteo, Bright Sky (DWD), MET Norway — weather forecast for given coordinates; RainViewer — rain radar images;
  • CelesTrak — GNSS satellite orbits; NOAA SWPC — geomagnetic Kp index;
  • Mapzen terrain tiles on Amazon S3 — terrain for the 3D view;
  • jsDelivr (CDN) — libraries for some tools (PDF export, OCR) when first used;
  • GitHub Pages — hosting of the app; Cloudflare — the app's server (see above).

After saving data for offline use the app works without a connection; usage records and error reports are sent when there is a signal again.

Exports and sharing

Data exports (CSV, DXF, GeoJSON, .argeo…) and sharing via QR code are always started by you, and the data goes only where you send it.

Children

QTRIG is a professional tool for surveyors and is not directed at children.

Your rights

You remove data stored on the device by clearing the app's / browser's data, and data on the server by deleting your account (see above). You can also ask the operator for a copy, correction or erasure of data stored on the server using the contact below; we handle requests without undue delay and within 30 days at the latest. If you use the app in a company, the company admin also decides about data in the company space. You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in the Czech Republic: the Office for Personal Data Protection, uoou.gov.cz).
